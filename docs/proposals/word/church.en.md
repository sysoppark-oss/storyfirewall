Living Waters Church (맑은물속교회)
Pyeongchang, Korea · Korea Baptist Convention

We are a Baptist church in Pyeongchang, Gangwon Province, Korea. We believe that only the gospel itself, not impressive programs or human methods, changes a person at the root. The church was founded to guard the pure gospel, with nothing mixed in, the truth that is like clear water giving life to dying souls, and to let it flow out into the world.

Three callings
1. Be immersed deep in the clear water — a disciple's life fully under the authority of the Word (Hebrews 4:12, 2 Timothy 1:13)
2. Guard the purity of the clear water — a breakwater that keeps the gospel pure (2 Timothy 1:14)
3. Let the life of the clear water flow out — passing on the life we received to our neighbors and the next generation (Psalm 103:22, 2 Timothy 2:2)

Why we made this program
So that the gospel we received would not stay inside the church walls. We use our experience in networking and security to protect our neighbors, and we send Scripture along with the program. It is free for everyone, and nothing recorded on this PC is sent anywhere.
