This program was made by Living Waters Church in Pyeongchang, Korea (Korea Baptist Convention), and is given freely to everyone.

Living Waters Church is called to be immersed deep in the clear water of God's Word, to guard the purity of the gospel, and to let its life flow out into the world. This program is one of the streams flowing beyond the walls of the church.

We pray that the Holy Spirit will make us witnesses of Jesus Christ from Pyeongchang to Samaria and to the ends of the earth, and we offer this program on that road. May the Scripture verses throughout this program become a way to know him.

Nothing recorded on this PC is sent anywhere, and the source code is open (AGPL-3.0).
